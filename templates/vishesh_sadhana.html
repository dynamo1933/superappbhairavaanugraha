🔱 Sacred Sadhana

Kāmākhyā–Bhairava

Vishesh Sãdhana Guidelines — His Grace on us!

॥ भैरव अनुग्रह ॥

🔱 Bhairava Anugraha 🔱

This sacred sadhana can be performed on four of the most potent and spiritually charged nights of the year, each offering unique blessings and transformative power for Tantra sadhakas.

रक्तवर्णां त्रिनेत्रां च चन्द्रसूर्याग्निलोचनाम्।
स्मेरमुखीं वराभीति हस्तां कामाख्यां नमाम्यहम्॥
Raktavarnām trinetrām ca candrasūryāgnilocanām,
Smeramukhīm varābhīti hastām Kāmākhyām namāmyaham.
— I bow to Kāmākhyā, red in complexion, three-eyed, with moon, sun and fire as eyes, with a smiling face and hands bestowing boons and fearlessness.

Practicing this sadhana on any of these nights allows the sadhaka to connect deeply with the divine energies, awaken hidden spiritual potential, and receive the protective and transformative grace of Maa Kāmākhyā and Bhairava.

🌙

Four Sacred Nights for Sadhana

Choose any of these spiritually charged nights to perform this powerful sadhana

Maha Shivaratri
The night when Lord Shiva's consciousness is most accessible. Awakens inner wisdom, purification, and divine grace, dissolving past karmas.
Holika Dahan (Chhoti Holi)
The night of burning negativity and ego. Release inner impurities, attachments, and obstacles, invoking divine protection.
Naraka Chaturdaśī
The night of victory over inner darkness. Highly auspicious for Bhairava sadhana, helping conquer fears and negativity.
Kārtika Amāvasyā
The spiritually charged night of cosmic renewal. Magnifies spiritual purification, mantra potency, and the blessings of Shakti and Bhairava.

"These two nights — Naraka Chaturdaśī and Kārtika Amāvasyā — are the nights when the veil between life and death, light and shadow, is thinnest. The fire of Bhairava burns away ignorance, and the womb of Kāmākhyā receives all the fallen seeds of karma to transform them."

Time and Place

Sacred timing and space preparation

Place: Quiet, dimly lit area with ghee lamp and incense
🧘
Seat (Āsana): Black or red woolen cloth or kusha seat
🔱
Symbols: Śrī Kāmākhyā or Śrī Guru Bhairava Yantra

Pūrvasevā (Preparation) — Step by Step

Follow these sacred steps with devotion

1

Ācamana & Prānāyāma

आचमन एवं प्राणायाम

Purify the body and mind with the sacred mantras:

ॐ केशवाय स्वाहा | ॐ नारायणाय स्वाहा | ॐ माधवाय स्वाहा |
Om Keśavāya Svāhā | Om Nārāyanāya Svāhā | Om Mādhavāya Svāhā |

Finally, pour water into the right hand and release it through the gap between the middle and ring fingers:

ॐ गोविन्दाय नमः
Om Govindaya Namah
2

Bhūtaśuddhi (Inner Purification)

भूतशुद्धि

Visualize five flames within your body:

🔵
Head
Ākāśa - Blue
🟢
Chest
Vāyu - Green
🔴
Navel
Agni - Red
⚪
Thighs
Jala - White
🟡
Feet
Prthvī - Yellow

Merge all flames into the heart.

3

Āvāhana (Invocation)

आवाहन

Recite with deep connection and feeling:

ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं कामाख्यायै नमः | ॐ ह्रीं भैरवाय नमः ||
"Om Aim Hrīm Klīm Kāmākhyāyai Namah | Om Hrīm Bhairavāya Namah ||"

Sprinkle water on yantra, lamp, and your heart.

4

Anganyāsa

अंगन्यास

Using Bhairava Tantra mantra form:

ॐ भैरवाय हृदयाय नमः |
ॐ रुद्राय शिरसे स्वाहा |
ॐ कालभैरवाय शिखायै वषट् |
ॐ कामाख्यायै कवचाय हुं |
ॐ कालीयै नेत्र-त्रयाय वषट् |
ॐ त्रिपुरायै अस्त्राय फट् ||
5

Karananyāsa

करन्यास
ॐ ह्रीं अंगुष्ठाभ्यां नमः |
ॐ क्लीं तर्जनीभ्यां नमः |
ॐ ऐं मध्यमाभ्यां नमः |
ॐ ह्रीं अनामिकाभ्यां नमः |
ॐ क्लीं कनिष्ठिकाभ्यां नमः |
ॐ ऐं करतलकरपृष्ठाभ्यां नमः ||
🧘

Dhyāna (Meditation Verse)

Sacred visualization and contemplation

🔱 Sacred Visualization
Visualize Bhairava seated in cremation ground, surrounded by fire, and Mā Kāmākhyā as radiant red energy rising from the earth — merging into him.
"दिगम्बरा, भैरव-स्वरूपा, त्रिनेत्र, उग्रा, करुणामयी,
श्मशान-वासिनी, मा कामाख्या, देहि मे ज्ञानम्, मोक्षम्, अनुग्रहम्।"
"Digambarā, Bhairava-svarūpā, Trinetra, Ugrā, Karunāmayī,
Śmaśāna-vāsinī, Mā Kāmākhyā, Dehi me Jñānam, Moksam, Anugraham."
🍈

Bali & Tattva Offerings

Symbolic offering — representing transformation and light

🍈

White Ash Gourd (Safed Petha) or Lemon Bali

  1. Energize it with the mantra:
    ॐ क्षं भैरवाय बलिं समर्पयामि
    "Om Ksam Bhairavāya Balim Samarpayāmi"
  2. Touch it to your forehead, heart, and navel.
  3. Pierce with a trishula or cut with knife (Apply turmeric and Kumkum on each side) — visualizing all inner negativities released.
  4. Offer the juice/portion to the earth (outside or near a Peepal tree).
  5. Optional: Light a camphor on top of cut lemon during Arathi.
📝 Understanding the Symbolism
Lemon represents the solar tejas and neutralizes graha dosa, while Ash Gourd absorbs and dissolves tāmasika energies — hence used for protection and purification.

Japa & Main Mantras

Sacred mantras for invocation and blessing

🌸

Dhyāna Mantra for Kāmākhyā

ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं कामाख्यायै नमः
"Om Aim Hrīm Klīm Kāmākhyāyai Namah"
🔱

Tantra Mantra for Bhairava

ॐ ह्रीं बटुक भैरवाय स्वाहा
"Om Hrīm Batuka Bhairavāya Svāhā"

or

ॐ ह्रीं क्षं भैरवाय नमः
"Om Hrīm Ksam Bhairavāya Namah"
⚡

Combined Kaula Mantra (For Advanced Sādhaka)

ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं भैरव-कामाख्यायै नमः स्वाहा
"Om Aim Hrīm Klīm Bhairava-Kāmākhyāyai Namah Svāhā"

Japa Count: Minimum 108 or multiples of 3 (like 324)

🔔

Arādhana & Pūrnāhuti

Light camphor, ring bell or damaru and chant

ॐ भैरवाय नमः | ॐ कामाख्यायै नमः | अनुग्रहं देहि मे देवि |
"Om Bhairavāya Namah | Om Kāmākhyāyai Namah | Anugraham Dehi Me Devi |"

Offer red flowers, incense, and surrender with palms joined.
Sit in silent meditation for 10 minutes, letting the prāna settle in the heart.

🙏

Closing — Guru Parampara

Close your eyes and offer salutation to the lineage

Om Gurubhyo Namah
Om Matsyendranāthāya Namah
Om Gorakshanāthāya Namah
Om Jalandhar Nāthāya Namah
Om Kanifnathāya Namah
Om Charpatināthāya Namah
Om Nityānandānāthāya Namah
Om Param Guru Bhairavāya Namah
🔮 Final Visualization
Lineage of Nath Gurus forms a protective circle around the sadhaka. Shakti flows from the first Guru (Matsyendranāthāya) to your heart and third eye. Feel inner guidance, protection, and alignment.

🕉️ Closing Mantra

"Om Tat Sat Om Bhairava Kāmākhyā Guru Paramparāyai Namah"

"Remember, this Naraka Chaturdaśī is not about outer darkness, it is the destruction of inner Naraka — ignorance, lust, pride. Kārtika Amāvasyā is not emptiness, but the womb of Kāmākhyā — where all dissolved seeds of karma are reborn as light.

When you invoke Bhairava and Kāmākhyā together, you are invoking both the Fire and the Womb — Consciousness and Power merging as Liberation."

— Bhairava Anugraha
