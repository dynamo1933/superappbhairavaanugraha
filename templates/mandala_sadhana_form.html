The Four Pillars of Mandala Discipline (Niyama)

Read reverently before recording your inner commitment in the sacred register

I

Brahmacharya & Mental Purity

Conservation of vital prana through celibacy in thought, speech, and action throughout the vow cycle.

II

Sāttvic Diet & Ahara Śuddhi

Partake only in fresh, unpolluted, vegetarian offerings. Complete abstention from alcohol, intoxicants, and tamasic stimulants.

III

Unbroken Japa & Daily Āsana

Daily mantra offerings done at fixed Sandhyā hours. If a single day is broken, the cycle must be restarted with repentance.

IV

Silence & Devotional Surrender

Shield your practice from idle gossip and display. Hold the mantra within the cave of the heart in supreme stillness.
