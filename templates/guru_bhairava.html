Guru Bhairava

The Timeless Guru of Inner Flame — Beyond form, beyond knowledge, beyond time

॥ गुरु भैरव साधना पद्धति ॥

🕉️ The Primal Spark of Awakening 🕉️

On the sacred occasion of Guru Purnima, when the full moon reflects the brilliance of Guru Tattva across the cosmos, we bow to Guru Bhairava — He who is beyond form, beyond knowledge, beyond time. He is the primal spark of awakening, the stillness behind action, and the destroyer of falsehood.

भैरवो गुरुरेव हि, भैरवी शक्तिरूपिणी।
"Bhairavo Gururevahi, Bhairavi Shaktirūpiṇī"
— The Guru shines in Bhairava; in Bhairavi, his grace flows.

Let this be a living sadhana, not only for mantra or ritual — but for becoming the disciple worthy of the Eternal Guru.

Sadhana Structure: 3 / 5 / 21 Days

Choose your duration based on your inner calling:

3
Days

To receive blessings & clear emotional blocks

5
Days

To stabilize Guru Bhava and inner discipline

21
Days

To completely realign your inner self to the Guru within

You may begin this sacred practice on any Purnima, Navaratri, or especially on the auspicious night of Guru Purnima — or at any time, with a clear and heartfelt Sankalpa (sacred resolve).

🕉️ Steps for Sankalpa (Intention Declaration)

"I offer this Sadhana with love, surrender, and longing to dissolve my ego, purify my mind, and awaken to the Guru Tattva within. May Guru Bhairava accept me."

🔱 Dhyāna Mantra

ध्यायामि गुरुं भैरवं त्रिनेत्रं, शान्तं स्थाणुं स्वात्मप्रकाशम्।
दिगम्बरं मुक्तकेशं प्रसन्नं, हृदि स्थितं ज्ञानमयं विभुं च॥
Dhyāyāmi Guruṁ Bhairavaṁ Trinetraṁ, Śāntaṁ Sthāṇuṁ Svātmaprakāśam।
Digambaraṁ Muktakeśaṁ Prasannaṁ, Hṛdi Sthitaṁ Jñānamayaṁ Vibhuṁ Cha॥

Guru Bhairava Sadhana – Step by Step

1

Guru Prarthana (Invoking the Guru)

गुरु प्रार्थना

Sit in stillness. Fold hands and chant:

गुरुर् ब्रह्मा गुरुर् विष्णुः गुरुर् देवो महेश्वरः।
गुरुः साक्षात् परब्रह्म तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः॥
"Gurur Brahmā Gurur Viṣhṇuḥ Gurur Devo Maheśwaraḥ
Guruḥ Sākṣhāt Parabrahma Tasmai Śhrī Gurave Namaḥ"
🔱 Visualization
Visualize Bhairava as the Guru — black as midnight, holding trishula and damaru, seated in supreme stillness. He is your breath, your conscience, your mirror.
2

Lighting the Deepam and Dhoopam

दीपम् धूपम्

Light the lamp and incense facing East or North. Chant:

Mantra (1 Mala - 108 times)
ॐ कालभैरवाय नमः
"Om Kālabhairavāya Namaḥ"

As the smoke rises, feel your thoughts and desires being carried to the Guru. Let the fire consume your impurities.

3

Guru Dakshina – Surrender of Pancha Vikaras

गुरु दक्षिणा

Before mantra japa, offer these five inner enemies to the Guru:

Take 5 black sesame seeds and with each, speak:

"O Bhairava, I surrender my [Desire / Anger / Attachment / Greed / Ego] at your feet. Burn it in your fire. Let only your will remain in me."

💘
Desire
Kama (काम)
🔥
Anger
Krodha (क्रोध)
🔗
Attachment
Moha (मोह)
💰
Greed
Lobha (लोभ)
👑
Ego
Ahamkara (अहंकार)

Let this be your true dakshina, more than any gift or offering.

4

Dhyanam – Guru Bhairava Meditation

ध्यानम्

Close your eyes. Visualize Bhairava sitting in shmashana (cremation ground), radiating silence and compassion, with dogs at his side, surrounded by the fire of transformation.

🌬️ Breath Dharana (Sarvavijñāna Bhairava Tattva)
Inhale (Puraka): "He is within me" | Saḥ mayi antargataḥ asti.
Exhale (Rechaka): "I am within Him" | Ahaṁ tasmin antargataḥ asmi

Feel the boundary between you and Him dissolve.

5

Japa – Guru Bhairava Mantra Chanting

जप

Choose from the following mantras:

🔸 Beginner Mantra (Simple & Powerful)
ॐ बं भैरवाय नमः।
"Om Baṁ Bhairavāya Namaḥ"

108 or 1008 times

🔶 Guru Bhairava Dhyana Mantra (Advanced)
ॐ नमो भगवते गुरु भैरवाय रुद्र रूपाय,
कालाय महाक्षाय सर्वज्ञानाय स्वाहा
"Om Namo Bhagavate Guru Bhairavāya Rudra Rūpāya,
Kālāya Mahākṣāya Sarvajñānāya Svāhā"

Chant with rhythm and bhava — not for result, but as surrender.

6

Bhava Arpana – Emotional Offering

भाव अर्पण

From your heart, offer your essence:

"O Guru Bhairava, I am yours. You are not just my teacher — you are my very breath, my guide through darkness, the mirror of my soul."

If inspired, sing a Kalabhairava Astakam or just remain in silent presence.

7

Naivedyam and Final Offering

नैवेद्यम्

Offer simple Naivedyam or as you wish to:

🥛 Milk
🍎 Fruit
🌿 Tulsi water
🍬 Black sesame sweets
🍃 Any sattvic food

Conclude with gratitude, knowing the Guru's grace is ever-present.

"The Guru does not give you anything new. He simply removes the veil of ignorance, revealing the light that has always been within you."

— Ancient Wisdom of Guru Bhairava Tradition
