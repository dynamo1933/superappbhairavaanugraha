PRĀṆA PRATIṢṬHĀNA

॥ दैव अनुग्रह - भैरव प्रतिष्ठा ॥

The Living Consciousness

Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhāna is the sacred alchemical process of breathing life into a form. Through this ancient ritual, a Śiva Liṅga is transformed from a physical stone into a Cit-Śakti (Conscious Field) — a living portal to the heart of Bhairava. It is the bridge between the unmanifested Brahman and the living presence in your home.

The Consecration Mahāmantra

asya śrī-kālabhairava-liṅga-prāṇa-pratiṣṭhāna-mahāmantrasya
śrī-kālabhairava ṛṣiḥ | anuṣṭup chandaḥ | śrī-kālabhairavaḥ devatā |
hrīṁ bījaṁ | klīṁ śaktiḥ | saṁ kīlakaṁ | liṅga-prāṇa-pratiṣṭhāne viniyogaḥ ‖

🔱 Punaḥ Prāṇa Sthāpana (Renewal)

For existing Liṅgas that feel stagnant, this renewal process resets the energetic core. It is ideally performed during Pradoṣa, Amāvāsyā, or Kālāṣṭamī. The process begins with a 24-hour water immersion (Jalādhivāsa) to clear prior impressions.

"From this moment onward, know that He now dwells within your home — not as a symbol, but as a living presence of Śiva-Bhairava Himself."

Treat Him with the same love, reverence, and care that you offer to your own self. Whatever is prepared — be it food, water, or any offering — let it first be dedicated to Him. When you serve Him, you are not merely offering food or flowers — you are offering your gratitude, your breath, and your being.

May Bhairava’s grace guide your steps and illuminate your path!

॥ ॐ भैरवाय नमः ॐ कामाख्यै नमः ॥

DAY 0

Preparation & Sacred Resolve

Materials Needed

  • Śiva Liṅga (Bhairava Swarūpam)
  • Copper or brass Pañcapātra & udharani
  • Rudrākṣa mālā
  • Bilva leaves, flowers, sandal paste, honey, milk, yogurt, ghee, sugar
  • Dhūpa, dīpa, camphor
  • White cloth for altar
  • Ganga or consecrated water
  • Rice, turmeric, betel nut, incense sticks

Altar Preparation

  • Clean the area with water and Ganga-jala.
  • Spread a clean white or red cloth.
  • Place the pīṭha in the center facing east.

Sankalpa (Sacred Resolve)

Hold water in your right palm and recite:

"Mama upātta samasta duritakṣaya dvārā Śrī Parameśvara prītyartham Pañca ahorātra paryantam Śrī Śiva Liṅga Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhānam kariṣye."

(For the pleasure of Mahadeva, I perform this 5-day consecration to invoke life into this linga.)

DAY 1

Bhūta Śuddhi & Āvāhana

Purpose: Purify yourself and the linga's material body to become a vessel for Śiva consciousness.

1. Ācamana & Prāṇāyāma

Perform 3 rounds of deep inhalation/exhalation. Mentally chant:

Om Apavitraḥ Pavitro Vā Sarvāvasthāṁ Gato'pi Vā
Yaḥ Smaret Puṇḍarīkākṣaṁ Sa Bāhyābhyantaraḥ Śuciḥ Om Śuciḥ Om Śuciḥ.

2. Bhūta Śuddhi (Purification of Elements)

Visualize your body and the linga filled with light from mūlādhāra to sahasrāra. Chant:

Om Ham Pṛthivyātmane Namaḥ
Earth Element
Om Vam Jalātmane Namaḥ
Water Element
Om Ram Agnyātmane Namaḥ
Fire Element
Om Yam Vāyvātmane Namaḥ
Air Element
Om Lam Ākāśātmane Namaḥ
Space Element

3. Āvāhana (Invocation)

Sprinkle Ganga-jala on lingam and recite:

Om Aim Hrīṁ Śrīṁ Śivāya Namaḥ | Āvāhaya āvāhaya Mahādeva Mahāliṅge Nivāsāya Namaḥ ‖

4. Dhyānam

Visualize a radiant pillar of light emerging from the linga — endless above and below. Chant:

“Ādiyanta rahitaṁ jyotiḥ liṅgarūpaṁ Mahēśvaram
Dhyāyēd devasya rūpēṇa sarvabhūtāntarātmanaḥ.”

This mantra to be recited everyday for 11, 21, 45, 54 or 108 days (1 or more malas) and Kalabhairava Astakam on end of everyday closure.
DAY 2

Mantra Nyāsa & Bīja Śakti

Purpose: To install mantra and subtle energy currents (nāḍī śakti) within the linga.

1. Pañcabrahma Nyāsa

Touch the Liṅga in five directions while chanting:

  • West: Om Sadyojātāya Namaḥ
  • North: Om Vāmadevāya Namaḥ
  • South: Om Aghorāya Namaḥ
  • East: Om Tatpuruṣāya Namaḥ
  • Upward: Om Īśānāya Namaḥ
Visualize each direction glowing with corresponding Śiva aspect.

2. Mūla Mantra Japa

Chant "Om Namaḥ Śivāya" 108 times with deep awareness. Offer one bilva leaf with each repetition if possible.

3. Bīja Śakti Sthāpana

Mentally inscribe bījas into the linga. Visualize the Chakra getting activated:

Ham (Śiva Tattva) | Sam (Śakti Tattva) | Śam (Jñāna) | Vam (Kriyā) | Yam (Icchā)

Then seal with:

Om Namaḥ Śivāya, Liṅge Prāṇaṁ Pratiṣṭhāpayāmi.

DAY 3

Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhā & Jīva Āvāhana

Purpose: Invoke the living consciousness (prāṇa śakti) of Lord Śiva into the linga.

1. Nādī Prāṇāyāma

Inhale deeply chanting "So...", Exhale chanting "...Ham". 11 rounds to awaken your inner prāṇa.

2. Prāṇa Pratiṣṭhā

Place your right hand over the linga (like a mudrā of blessing) and chant slowly:

Om Hrīṁ Śrīṁ Klīṁ Aim Om Śivāya Prāṇaḥ Pratiṣṭhāpayāmi Svāhā ‖

Visualize liquid gold light flowing from your heart, through your palm, and anchoring into the core of the Liṅga.

3. Āvāhana & Nyāsa

  • Om Śivāya Ātmānaṁ Samarpayāmi (offer your soul)
  • Om Bhairavāya Prāṇān Samarpayāmi (offer your prāṇa)
  • Om Rudrāya Manaḥ Samarpayāmi (offer your mind)
  • Om Mahādeva Namaḥ - Tiṣṭha Tiṣṭha Sthirō Bhava
DAY 4

Abhiṣeka & Sodaśopacāra Pūjā

Purpose: Honor the invoked deity through worship, adornment, and sustained mantra resonance.

1. Abhiṣeka with Pañcāmrita

Chant each line as you pour:

  • 🥛 Milk: Om Soma Sūryāgnilocana Śivāya Namaḥ
  • 🥣 Curd: Om Gaurīvallabha Śivāya Namaḥ
  • 🧈 Ghee: Om Trilocanāya Namaḥ
  • 🍯 Honey: Om Rudrāya Namaḥ
  • 🍬 Sugar: Om Nīlakaṇṭhāya Namaḥ

2. Alankāra (Decoration)

Apply sandal paste, decorate with bilva leaves, and light lamps around. Offer flowers chanting:

Om Puṣpāñjaliṁ Samarpayāmi.

3. 108 or 1008 Mantra Japa

Chant "Om Namaḥ Śivāya".
Optional: For Bhairava aspect — Om Hrīṁ Baṭuka Bhairavāya Namaḥ

DAY 5

Completion & Energy Sealing

Purpose: Seal the energy, express gratitude, and ensure the linga becomes a permanent spiritual field.

1. Pūrnāhuti & Dīpa Ārati

Offer ghee lamp with chanting:

Om Namo Rudrāya, Om Namo Bhavāya, Om Namo Sarvātmane Mahādeva Namaḥ.

2. Prārthanā

“Yatra Liṅgaṁ Tatra Śivaḥ, Yatra Śivaḥ Tatra Śubham.”

Pray for peace, protection, and guidance.

3. Stambhana Mudrā (Energy Sealing)

Place both palms together before the Liṅga and say:

Om Anantāya Namaḥ | Om Ānandāya Namaḥ | Om Śivāya Namaḥ ‖

Visualize the light settling into stillness.

4. Prasāda Distribution

Offer naivedya, perform simple ārati, and share prasāda among family.

After the 5 Days

  • Continue daily abhiṣeka with water and chant "Om Namaḥ Śivāya" 108 times.
  • Every Monday or Pradoṣa, offer bilva leaves and light a ghee lamp.
  • The linga is now Prāṇa-Pratiṣṭhita — a living seat of Śiva-Śakti.

Closing Mantra

(To be recited each time you end worship)

Ananta Kalyāṇa Guṇātmakam Śivam
Śaśāṅka Kāntam Kamalāpatim Harim
Viśuddha Vigraham Acalam Nirāmayam
Namāmi Śambhum Śivam Īśam Īśvaram.
