✦ Who can practice Bhairava Sādhana?

Bhairava Sadhana is accessible to any sincere seeker devoted to truth, courage, and self-purification. While advanced tantric rituals require formal initiation (Deeksha) from a lineage Guru, foundational practices such as the Bhairava Gayatri, lighting of mustard or sesame oil lamps on Ashtami, and namasankirtan are open to all householders.

✦ What is the significance of lighting oil lamps for Bhairava?

Lighting a lamp with pure sesame oil (Til oil) or mustard oil (Sarson tel) dissipates dense, stagnant energetic blockages (Tamas). The flame symbolizes the awakening of Agni Tattva within the Sushumna nadi, dissolving fears, anxieties, and negative planetary influences of Rahu and Shani.
