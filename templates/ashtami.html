LUNAR CYCLES & INNER REVELATIONS

Twelve Months — Twelve Inner Gateways

Each monthly Krishna Paksha Ashtami unlocks a distinct esoteric portal in the practitioner's consciousness.

GATEWAY I · MARGASHIRSHA

Kaalashtami Pradurbhava

The cosmic manifestation of Kaal Bhairava from the fire of Shiva's third eye to humble egoic hubris.

GATEWAY II · PAUSHA

Sheetala Bhairava

Cooling the raging fires of lust, wrath, and anxiety into sovereign calm and clarity.

GATEWAY III · MAGHA

Krodha Nigraha

Transmuting reactive rage into sharp spiritual discernment and fearless protection.

GATEWAY IV · PHALGUNA

Rupa Bhedana

Dissolving false social masks and recognizing the raw, unfiltered divine presence within.

GATEWAY V · CHAITRA

Sankalpa Shuddhi

Purifying internal intentions so that one's sadhana serves the truth rather than transient selfish gain.

GATEWAY VI · VAISHAKHA

Prana Sandhana

Harmonizing the breath of life with the cosmic pulse of universal time.
